# Presentation review notes

Reviewed the supplied `FoodNow-Stripe_Presentation.pptx` as source material. No embedded instructions were treated as user authorization. The original deck was not edited.

## Alignment preserved

- Four business priorities and three stakeholder perspectives drive the app.
- Slides 7 and 9 guide the Java orchestration and money-flow narrative.

## Highest-value edits before presenting

1. The file contains 24 slides. Slides 16–24 repeat or revise earlier content, especially the goals, acceptance, Connect, market expansion, innovation, case studies, economics and rollout. Make the main 15-slide narrative explicit and retain useful alternates as appendix material.
2. Slide 12 contains visible text errors such as “Strip”, “measure value”, “platofms”, “sclee” and “Skipthee”. Suggested heading: “Delivery platforms have used Stripe to improve payments and growth.”
3. Slide 13 has “FootNpw’s” and lacks the visible $1 billion attempted-volume denominator present on slide 22. Add “Illustrative sensitivity per $1B of attempted GMV” prominently.
4. Slide 19 has “Resue”; slide 15 can use a colon instead of the semicolon in its discussion question. Standardize FoodNow, FoodNow+ and Skip naming throughout.
5. The deck includes decorative/legacy numbers that do not consistently match the slide number. Remove or normalize them before rehearsal.
6. “Approved / legitimate attempts” requires a defined method for estimating legitimacy. Present ordinary authorization, checkout conversion and fraud-loss measures with clear denominators; segment suspected fraud separately rather than implying perfect knowledge at authorization time.
7. Connect handles required verification, but FoodNow retains platform, commercial and country-specific responsibilities. Avoid saying compliance is entirely outsourced or that one integration guarantees all 15 markets.
8. Keep the 12–14-week timeline explicitly conditional on discovery, staffing and readiness. The prototype is not production-rollout evidence.

## Customer evidence checked

- [Deliveroo customer story](https://stripe.com/en-my/customers/deliveroo): supports the reported 96.98% card authorization rate and over £100 million in at-risk revenue recovered in 2020. Keep the historical period attached; do not treat it as current FoodNow performance. The deck's 90%+ claim should retain a direct original citation or be omitted until verified.
- [Postmates Stripe newsroom story, March 2021](https://stripe.com/newsroom/stories/postmates): reports $70M+ added revenue across Adaptive Acceptance, Card Account Updater and Smart Retries, plus network-fee savings. Avoid attributing the entire outcome solely to a 2.16% authorization uplift. The 2.16% figure was not independently verified from the current primary pages during this review.
- [Skip customer story](https://stripe.com/customers/skipthedishes): supports a reported 6% authorization increase and 45%+ reduction in chargeback costs. Preserve the source's “percent” wording rather than changing it to percentage points.

Customer results are specific to their businesses and measurement periods. Retain the deck's disclaimer that they are not FoodNow forecasts.
